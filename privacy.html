NYX Backup Privacy Policy

NYX Backup is a private, single-user backup system.

Google Drive access is used only to create, read, verify, restore, and manage the owner's encrypted backup repository.

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for analytics.

Authentication credentials and backup operations are processed locally by the owner's systems using rclone and Restic.

Backup contents are encrypted by Restic before being stored in Google Drive.
